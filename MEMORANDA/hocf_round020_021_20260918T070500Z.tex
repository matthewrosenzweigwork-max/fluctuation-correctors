\paragraph{Common model.}

\[
 dX_i=\frac1N\sum_{j\ne i}K(X_i-X_j)\,dt+\sqrt{2\nu_N}\,dW_i,

 \widehat g(k)=c_{d,s}|k|^{s-d}\ (k\ne0),\quad\widehat g(0)=0,
\]
with coefficient-one Riesz normalization and Fourier characters $e^{2\pi i k\cdot x}$.
Here $c_{d,s}=\pi^{s-d/2}\Gamma((d-s)/2)/\Gamma(s/2)$, $d\ge3$ and $0<s\le d-2$. Write
$\eta_N=N^{-1}\sum_i\delta_{X_i}$, $\rho_N=\eta_N-dx$,
$b(0)=1$, $b(\nu)=\min(1,1/\nu)$ for $\nu>0$, and $\sigma_N=\sqrt{Nb(\nu_N)}$.
The empirical and background terms use ordered deleted labels divided by $N^k$, and $P_N=U_2/2$. Translation invariance and pathwise uniqueness give exact one-body Haar centering. No positive-time product law is assumed.

\paragraph{The continuous-path assertion.}

\paragraph{The simultaneous source estimate.}

\]
where $R=(d-s)/2+d+5$, $\|f\|_{\mathcal A_R}=\sum_k(1+|k|)^R|\widehat f(k)|$, and the bracketed quantity is nonnegative. The exact retained-energy identity and actual expected-energy bound imply
\[
 \sup_{r\le T}\E[E_\ell+S_{2\ell}+c_{2\ell}](X(r))

\]
Every spatial seminorm of $Q_a^{\nu_N}h$ is bounded by the corresponding seminorm of $h$. Taking $\ell=N^{-2/d}$ therefore bounds the entire backward-test family at once. The actual nonlinear remainder

\paragraph{Actual tightness and the continuous Gaussian law.}
The genuine stopped first-order identity gives $Z_N=I_N+M_N+E_N$, with
\[
 I_N^h(t)=\sqrt{b_N/N}\sum_i\left(Q_t^{\nu_N}h(X_i(0))-\int h\right),\qquad
 M_N^h(t)=\sqrt{2\nu_Nb_N/N}\sum_i\int_0^t
       \nabla Q_{t-r}^{\nu_N}h(X_i(r))\cdot dW_i(r).
\]
The process $M_N^h$ is a stochastic convolution in its terminal time. Its increment between $u$ and $t$ retains both the change of the old integrand on $[0,u]$ and the new integral on $(u,t]$.
The former integrand is bounded by $C|t-u|$ and the latter by $C$, uniformly on bounded noise intervals. A deterministic bracket-density bound and stopped It\^o calculus yield
\[
 \E|M_N^h(t)-M_N^h(u)|^4\le C|t-u|^2,
 \qquad \E|I_N^h(t)-I_N^h(u)|^4\le C|t-u|^4.
\]
Initial fourth moments are bounded. Dyadic adjacent-increment estimates and chaining give compact H\"older sets for $I_N+M_N$. The vanishing expected supremum of $E_N$ transfers tightness by actual modulus sets, with finitely many small $N$ handled by continuity. A fixed neighborhood of a compact set is not presumed compact.

For a real orthonormal sine/cosine basis $(e_l)$, independent standard normals $\xi_l$ and independent Brownian motions $B_l$ construct the limit through
\[
 Z_j(t)=\sum_l\left(\int h_je_l\right)\sqrt{\bar b}
 \left[e^{-L_lt}\xi_l+\sqrt{2\bar\nu a_l}\int_0^t e^{-L_l(t-r)}dB_l(r)\right].
\]
The deterministic convolution identity bounds its path supremum by twice the Brownian supremum. Smooth Fourier coefficients give absolute uniform convergence almost surely and convergence of tails in expected uniform norm. The series thus defines the continuous Gaussian process with the displayed covariance. Actual finite-dimensional convergence, polygonal interpolation, tight moduli and compact-set Lipschitz approximation prove full weak convergence for bounded continuous path functionals.
