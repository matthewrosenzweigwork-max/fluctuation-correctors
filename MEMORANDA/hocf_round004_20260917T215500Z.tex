\section{The radial comparison}
Fix an integer $d\ge3$, $0<s\le d-2$, $p=s+2$, $N\ge2$,
$0\le\nu<\infty$, $0\le T<\infty$, and a real symmetric matrix $A$.
On $D=\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}$, put
\[
 v_N(z)=\frac{2s}{N}|z|^{-s-2}z,\quad
 L=2\nu\Delta+v_N\cdot\nabla,\quad
 j_0(z)=s|z|^{-s},\quad J_A(z)=j_0(z)\theta^TA\theta,
 \qquad\theta=z/|z|.
\]
The terminal equation is $(\partial_t+L)\Phi=-J_A$, with
$\Phi(T,\cdot)=0$. The coefficient $2\nu$ is the relative diffusion,
corresponding to noise $\sqrt{4\nu}$. Define
\[
 F_{N,\tau}(r)=\frac N4\left[
 (r^p+2sp\tau/N)^{2/p}-r^2\right],\qquad r>0.
\]
For $q=r^p/(r^p+2sp\tau/N)$, direct differentiation gives
\begin{align}
 \partial_\tau F-v_N\cdot\nabla F&=sr^{-s},\\
 \Delta F&=\frac N2\left[q^{s/p}(d+s-sq)-d\right].\label{lap}
\end{align}
The derivative of $q^{s/p}(d+s-sq)$ is
\[
 \frac{s}{p}q^{s/p-1}[d+s-(2s+2)q]\ge0,
\]
since $d\ge s+2$. Its value at $q=1$ is $d$, so
$-Nd/2\le\Delta F\le0$, strictly negative for positive remaining time.
Consequently
\[
 (\partial_t+L)F_{N,T-t}=-j_0+2\nu\Delta F\le-j_0.
\]
Writing $K=\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}$, this is a radial majorant for the
signed source, because $|J_A|\le K j_0$. The radial amplitude satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{bounds}
 0\le F_{N,\tau}(r)\le
 \min\left\{\frac{(2sp)^{2/p}}4N^{s/p}\tau^{2/p},\ s\tau r^{-s}\right\}.
\end{equation}
No small-diffusion assumption enters this inequality. The anisotropic
transport solution itself need not be superharmonic.

\section{Construction, collision avoidance and source integrability}
Construct the path solving
\[
 Z_h=z+\sqrt{4\nu}B_h+\int_0^h v_N(Z_a)\,da
\]
by bounded Lipschitz cutoffs of the drift, stopped on increasing annuli.
For each cutoff, Picard increments on $[0,H]$ are bounded by
$B_mL_m^kH^{k+1}/(k+1)!$. Their uniform convergence gives existence;
the same factorial estimate gives uniqueness. The resulting solution
map is continuous in its initial point and continuous driving path for
the cutoff equation. Countable patching gives a Borel maximal map,
with lifetime the limit of the annular exit times.

The following It\^o calculations stop inside a finite annulus.
All derivatives and coefficients of the stochastic integrals are bounded there.
For $V(r)=r^2+r^{-(d-2)}$,
\[
 LV=4\nu d+\frac{4s}{N}r^{-s}
       -\frac{2s(d-2)}N r^{-s-d}\le C_{N,\nu},
\]
where an explicit admissible constant is
\[
 C_{N,\nu}=4\nu d+
 \frac{4sd}{N(s+d)}
 \left[\frac{2s}{(d-2)(s+d)}\right]^{s/d}.
\]
If $\rho_{a,R}$ is the first exit from $a<|z|<R$, expectation of
stopped It\^o's formula gives
\[
 \Prob_z(\rho_{a,R}\le H)
 \le(a^{d-2}+R^{-2})[V(z)+C_{N,\nu}H].
\]
Taking $a\downarrow0$, $R\uparrow\infty$ in nested annuli proves
noncollision and nonexplosion from each nonzero starting point. It
also identifies the patched global path, rather than merely extracting
a subsequential law. The constant here need not be uniform in $\nu$.

For a fixed remaining time $\tau$, stopped It\^o applied to
$F_{N,\tau-h}(|Z_h|)$ gives
\[
 \E_zF_{N,\tau-\tau\wedge\rho}(|Z_{\tau\wedge\rho}|)
 +\E_z\int_0^{\tau\wedge\rho}j_0(Z_h)\,dh
 \le F_{N,\tau}(|z|).
\]
The first term is nonnegative. Monotone convergence of the positive
source integrals, after noncollision has been established, proves
\begin{equation}\label{occupation}
 \E_z\int_0^\tau j_0(Z_h)\,dh\le F_{N,\tau}(|z|).
\end{equation}
This supplies expectation integrability, which would not follow from
pathwise avoidance alone. The independent reconstruction also obtains
the sharper separate exit bounds
\[
 \Prob_z(\rho_{a,R}\le H,|Z_\rho|=a)\le(a/|z|)^{d-2},
\]
\[
 \Prob_z(\rho_{a,R}\le H,|Z_\rho|=R)
 \le\frac{|z|^2+4\nu dH+(4/N)F_{N,H}(|z|)}{R^2}.
\]
They follow by differentiating $r^{2-d}$ and $r^2$, respectively.

\section{The precise solution class and domain passage}
The absolutely integrable potential
\[
 U_A(\tau,z)=\E_z\int_0^\tau J_A(Z_h)\,dh,
 \qquad\Phi_A(t,z)=U_A(T-t,z)
\]
is jointly Borel: time integration of the measurable path map followed
by integration over Brownian paths preserves measurability; positive
and negative parts are finite by \eqref{occupation}. Moreover,
\begin{equation}\label{comparison}
 |U_A(\tau,z)|\le K F_{N,\tau}(|z|).
\end{equation}
This is a global bounded function for fixed $N,T$; its supremum need
not be uniform in $N$.

Pathwise uniqueness gives the deterministic flow identity under time
shifts. Brownian independent increments then give the deterministic-time
Markov property. Measurability permits conditioning on the random
restart point: the exceptional future path set has zero probability
for each fixed starting point, so its conditional integral is zero.
Truncation and \eqref{occupation} extend this conditioning to the source.
For each starting $(t,z)$,
\[
 \Phi_A(t+h,Z_h)+\int_0^hJ_A(Z_a)\,da
 =\E_z\left[\left.\int_0^{T-t}J_A(Z_a)\,da\right|\mathcal F_h\right].
\]
Thus the left side is a true, uniformly integrable martingale.
The claimed solution class consists exactly of bounded Borel functions
with zero terminal value and this true-martingale property from every
starting point. Taking terminal expectations proves uniqueness in this
class. No differentiability or origin trace is part of its definition.

For $\nu>0$, killing at annular exit gives the unique bounded killed
martingale potential
\[
 U_A^{a,R}(\tau,z)=\E_z\int_0^{\tau\wedge\rho_{a,R}}J_A(Z_h)\,dh.
\]
It obeys the same radial bound. On nested annuli the signed source
integrals converge almost surely and in $L^1$, dominated by the full
integrable absolute source integral. Their expectations therefore converge
to $U_A$ at each fixed time and point. This proves the specified domain
passage. Classical interior regularity is not inferred. Any bounded
classical solution meeting the stopped-It\^o and terminal-continuity
hypotheses is identified with this potential by the same $L^1$ passage.

When $\nu=0$, the path is the outward characteristic
$(|z|^p+2sp h/N)^{1/p}\theta$. The potential equals
$(\theta^TA\theta)F_{N,\tau}(|z|)$ and is unique in the pointwise forward
absolutely-continuous characteristic class. No extra annular outflow
condition is imposed. For $A=I$, $\nu>0$ and $\tau>0$, dominated convergence
in the stopped equality gives the strict diagnostic
\[
 0<U_I(\tau,z)=F_{N,\tau}(|z|)
       +2\nu\E_z\int_0^\tau\Delta F_{N,\tau-h}(|Z_h|)\,dh
       <F_{N,\tau}(|z|).
\]
Here \eqref{lap} bounds the Laplacian in absolute value, so this passage
needs no uncontrolled derivative limit.

\section{The initial iid diagnostic}
Fix a smooth even cutoff $0\le\chi\le1$, equal to one near zero and
supported in an embedded torus ball $B_R$, $R<1/2$. Set
$H(x,y)=\chi(x-y)U_A(\tau,x-y)$, with any finite diagonal value.
Reflection symmetry makes this a real symmetric Borel pair kernel.
For iid labels of density $\mu$ with $\|\mu\|_\infty\le M$,
\[
 \|H\|_{L^2(\mu^2)}^2
 \le M\|\chi U_A\|_{L^2(\T^d)}^2
 \le MK^2\int_{B_R}F_{N,\tau}^2.
\]
There is one density factor because
$\int\mu(y+z)\mu(y)\,dy\le M$.
For $m=\mu^2(H)$, $q=H_\mu-m$, and
$H_c=H-m-q(x)-q(y)$, the exact mean-field-centered statistic is
\[
 P_N[H]=-\frac m{2N}-\frac1{N^2}\sum_iq(X_i)
            +\frac1{N^2}\sum_{i<j}H_c(X_i,X_j).
\]
Conditional centering and shared-label orthogonality give
\[
 \E P_N[H]^2=\frac{m^2}{4N^2}+\frac{\|q\|_2^2}{N^3}
       +\frac{N-1}{2N^3}\|H_c\|_2^2
 \le\frac{N-1}{2N^3}\|H\|_2^2.
\]
This retains the deterministic bias and first projection.
Put $b_N=\min(\beta_N,1)$ and $\sigma_N=\sqrt{Nb_N}$.
For $\ell=(2sp\tau/N)^{1/p}$, \eqref{bounds} yields
\[
 \int_{B_R}F^2\le |S^{d-1}|\left[
 \frac{N^2\ell^4\min(R,\ell)^d}{16d}
 +\mathbf1_{\ell<R}s^2\tau^2\int_\ell^Rr^{d-1-2s}\,dr\right].
\]
Consequently, for all $N\ge2$ and $\tau\in[0,T]$,
\[
 \sup_{0\le\tau\le T}\E|\sigma_NP_N[H]|^2
 \le C_T b_N
 \begin{cases}
 N^{-1},&2s<d,\\
 (1+\log N)/N,&2s=d,\\
 N^{-(d+2-s)/(s+2)},&2s>d.
 \end{cases}
\]
The constant is independent of finite $\nu\ge0$, $\beta_N>0$ and $N$;
$\nu=1/\beta_N$ is permitted. In the middle case
$\tau^2\log_+(1/\tau)$ is bounded at zero. A core larger than the cutoff
ball is controlled by the first term of the preceding exact bound.
The time supremum is outside the expectation. No lower bound for the
diffusive norm or path supremum is claimed.

\section{Remaining mathematical obligations}
The constructed diagnostic does not solve the full periodic pair equation.
Ordinary transport, both nonlocal responses, the actual time-dependent
backward test, the periodic force remainder and cutoff equation errors
still require a proof. Initial iid estimates do not transfer to the
evolved interacting law. The cubic residual, contractions, pair bracket,
singular generator passage and critical hierarchy tail remain open.
The full singularity range $0<s<d$ also extends beyond $s\le d-2$ here.

Throughout, the old condition $\beta_NN^{2s/d-1}\to0$ remains distinct
from full microscopic subcriticality
$\lambda_N=\beta_NN^{s/d-1}\to0$ and from microscopic criticality
$\lambda_N\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)$. This local estimate changes none of
those normalizations. The logarithmic case requires its separate convention.
The complete Markdown proofs, their immutable dossiers and the round report
supply the detailed constants and actual audit status.
