## B. Claims that must not be imported without repair

### OBS-001 — weighted positivity/lower bound

Pointwise nonnegativity of a symmetric weight \(w\) does not imply positive semidefiniteness of \(w(x,y)g(x-y)\). A universal weighted lower bound of the proposed form is therefore suspect and has a separated-support counterexample architecture. Reconstruct the exact claim and either prove a restricted version or record a counterexample.

### OBS-002 — “less singular” weighted remainder

A weight difference that vanishes to first order may combine with \(g'\) and a derivative-of-weight term to retain the original \(g\)-singularity. Perform the local Taylor expansion before assigning a lower singular order.

### OBS-003 — subtraction is not coercivity

Subtracting a transported crystalline or local-equilibrium energy can cancel a compression forcing algebraically. It does not by itself bound the centered local energy measure, traceless stress, shear response, or endpoint difference.

### OBS-004 — initial iid incompatibility

A nonzero crystalline coefficient at scale \(N^{s/d-1}\) is not automatically compatible with iid initial data. Compute the actual iid expectation and preparation layer before using a ground-state/local-Gibbs reference.

### OBS-005 — expectation is not concentration

Rotation symmetry or local Gibbs symmetry may cancel an expected traceless stress. It does not imply a probabilistic residual estimate, and conditioning on an exterior configuration need not preserve rotation invariance.

### OBS-006 — singular stochastic terms

For Coulomb/Riesz kernels, \(\Delta g\), diagonal delta terms, Itô traces, and martingales require explicit regularization. Harmonicity away from the diagonal does not remove distributional contributions.

### OBS-007 — midpoint stress

The exact force-divergence stress is bond-integrated. Midpoint formulas carry Taylor errors and torus coordinate issues. Do not use a midpoint approximation as an exact global identity.

### OBS-008 — predicted temperature gains are conditional

Any proposed range based on a conjectured mean commutator must be recomputed after centering, fluctuation, and dynamic remainder estimates. A mean asymptotic alone does not remove the temperature restriction.
