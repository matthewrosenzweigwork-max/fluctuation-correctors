## 4. Explicit uniform weighted estimate

\[

\[

## 5. Signed responses and the true full inverse

\[

\[

\[
C_{\alpha,q}=R^{-q}[(1+2^q)H_\alpha+2^\alpha B_q],
\quad W_q=\int w_q\le1+\frac{|\mathbb S^{d-1}|(2R)^{d-q}}{d-q}.
