statement, proof, source record, and limitation, not an optional appendix.

Use exactly the actual coefficient-one periodic d4 Coulomb process from
THM046/049 with independent Brownian drivers and iid Haar preparation.
Fix any N>=2 and any finite positive nu. The singular semigroup is
defined for every collision-free deterministic initial point by the
same actual path construction; its Haar integration uses the original
iid initial law.

The new conjunction is:

1. For every fixed Fourier mode k and finite T, the modal endpoint
   defect is exactly the sum of the two nonnegative terms in REPORT
   (3.1)--(3.2). The noise-response term equals the fixed-N heat-cutoff
   Dirichlet-cost limit (3.5). The cutoff martingale and cross brackets
   are (3.3)--(3.4), with no singular gradient implicitly inserted.
2. For an isolated pair parametrized by x1=z+r theta/2 and
   x2=z-r theta/2, with the other positions in any compact separated
   local domain Q as in REPORT section 4, there exist t0>0 and C<infinity
   depending on N,nu,k,Q such that the uniform directional limsup
   expansion (4.12) holds for every 0<t<=t0. The leading coefficient is
   4 pi^2 N^(-3/2) exp(2 pi i k.z) (k.theta)^2 sqrt(t), with its displayed
   negative sign in the semigroup response. No directional limit at
   collision is asserted.
3. For k=(1,0,0,0) and the real empirical cosine f, at every fixed
   N,nu there exists t0>0 such that for all 0<t<=t0, P_t^N f is not
   in W1,4 with respect to N-particle Haar measure. For the actual heat
   regularization, the fourth Haar moment of its full gradient has
   liminf +infinity as the cutoff tends to zero, at every such fixed t.
   Thus cutoff-uniform global C1 estimates, or cutoff-uniform Haar
   fourth-gradient bounds, are false even with arbitrary finite
   constants allowed to depend on N,nu,t.

The exact negation of this conjunction is a permitted finite N, positive
nu and datum violating at least one of these clauses, with each clause's
own quantifiers retained. In particular, failure of clause 2 means that
for some admitted k,Q every proposed finite C,t0 fails its uniform
limsup estimate at some time in (0,t0]; failure of clause 3 means there
is no positive interval on which its stated universal-in-time
nonmembership and cutoff-divergence conjunction holds.

The construction does not prove or negate the critical signed-correlation
limit, the original L1 source assertion, or the weaker response L2

N-uniform angular error, process limit, Gaussian law, alternate
