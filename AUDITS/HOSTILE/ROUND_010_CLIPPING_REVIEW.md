\(\alpha>0\); at large heat times it decays exponentially. The Fourier
coefficient is exactly
\[
\frac{A_{d,s}\Gamma(\alpha)}{(4\pi^2|k|^2)^\alpha}
=\pi^{s-d/2}\frac{\Gamma((d-s)/2)}{\Gamma(s/2)}|k|^{s-d},
\qquad k\ne0.

   \(2c_d(1-\delta_0)/N\), with both terms retained, not zero. This falsifies
